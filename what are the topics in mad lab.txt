📁 Android Resource Structure (Core Idea)

Android separates things into different folders:

Folder	Used For
res/layout/	UI design (buttons, text fields, structure)
res/values/	Data values (strings, colors, dimensions, arrays)
🎯 So what goes in strings.xml?

👉 Anything that is TEXT or DATA, not UI structure
